Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This page explains what data OROLADO (orolado.com) uses, why, and what your rights are.

1. Data we use

  • Account: e-mail address, display name, player number; if you sign in with Google, your Google e-mail address and name.
  • Operations: deposits, withdrawals, trades, bonuses and balances (demo and real).
  • Withdrawals: the details needed to pay you (for example a Mobile Money number or a crypto wallet address).
  • Security: IP address, device and browser type, sign-in dates, to protect accounts and prevent fraud.
  • Identity check: if we ask for an identity document before a withdrawal (see the Terms, Article 10), it is used only for that check.

2. Why

To create and manage your account, execute your trades, credit your deposits, pay your withdrawals, send you useful e-mails (codes, account information), protect the site against fraud and money laundering, and meet our legal obligations.

3. Who we share it with

Your data is never sold. It is only shared with the technical providers that run the site: hosting and database (Hostinger, Supabase), site security (Cloudflare), e-mail delivery (Brevo), Google sign-in (Google), payments (for example Flutterwave, OxaPay, Maketou), and advertising measurement (Meta / Facebook). Each one only receives what it needs.

4. Cookies and device storage

The site stores in your browser what it needs to work (sign-in, language, settings, recent history). An advertising measurement tool (Meta pixel) may also be loaded to know whether our ads work.

5. Retention

Data is kept while the account is active, then for the period required by law for financial operations.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or close your account, from the “Help” section of the site or by e-mail at support@orolado.com. Some operation records must be kept when the law requires it.

7. Minors

OROLADO is only for people aged 18 and over. We close any account opened by a minor.

8. Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords never stored in plain text, limited access to data.

9. Changes

This policy may change; the update date is shown at the top of this page.
