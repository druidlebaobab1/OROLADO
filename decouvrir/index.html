Beginner's guide

Tips, glossary and answers to common questions.

7 tips to get started
  1. Always start in demo. At least 50 trades with virtual money before going live.
  2. Only stake what you can afford to lose. Never money for rent, school or food.
  3. Small stakes, always the same. 1–2% of your balance per trade; doubling after a loss empties an account very fast.
  4. Set a daily limit and stop when you reach it.
  5. Don't trade when upset. After two or three losses, take a break.
  6. Prefer 1 minute at first. At 5 seconds, it's mostly luck.
  7. Write down your trades to see what works for you.
Glossary: candle, payout, volatility…
  1. Candle: shows the open, close, high and low of a period. Green = up, red = down.
  2. Stake: the amount placed on a trade.
  3. Payout: the gain as a % of the stake. 90% on $1 = $1.90 received.
  4. Duration: the time before the result, from 5 s to 10 min.
  5. Volatility: how strongly the price moves.
  6. Synthetic index: a market calculated continuously, open 24/7.
